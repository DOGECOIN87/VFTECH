Build on systems you own.

Start with the outcome

You don’t need to start with a platform or a technical specification. Start with what is getting in the way: a website you can’t change, a manual process that eats up your time, or accounts you don’t control.

VFTech helps you understand the problem, choose a sensible scope and rebuild on technology you own. The first conversation is about what is broken, what it costs and what a useful next step looks like.

Ownership is part of the design. Your code, accounts, data and documentation belong in your name from the start.

What you own

A handover should leave you with more than a working website. It should leave you able to keep running, make changes and bring in someone else when you need to.

The delivery path

The work moves through clear stages. You can see what has been agreed, what is being built and what needs your review.

  1. Conversation

    Understand the business, the friction and the goal.

  2. Assessment

    Look at the current systems and the realistic options.

  3. Proposal

    Agree the scope, price and boundaries in writing.

  4. Build

    Develop the system with useful previews and review points.

  5. Cutover

    Move across carefully and check the complete journey.

  6. Handover

    Transfer the code, accounts, data and documentation.

  7. Ongoing care

    Choose support if it is useful. It remains optional.

Read how we work for the full process.

Keep it useful

A system needs to keep working as the business changes. That can mean small improvements, maintenance or a more deliberate review of what is happening underneath.

AI can help with that work, while a person stays responsible for reviewing changes. It should support the business’s judgment and keep the systems understandable.

You can choose a monthly care plan or take the documented handover and continue independently. The next step starts with a conversation.
