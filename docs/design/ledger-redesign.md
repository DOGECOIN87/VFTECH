# Ledger / design 7

A back-office dashboard rather than another marketing landing page. Three equal summary columns lead into a 2:1 project ledger and review rail. The panel, sidebar and typography system carry through all seven service pages.

Typography: self-hosted Plus Jakarta Sans and DM Mono, with OFL licenses. This direction uses the newer vector master and existing logo choreography. The other designs are unchanged.

The homepage is explicitly a sample workspace. Six fictional projects support search, status filtering, project sorting and keyboard-accessible detail dialogs. Review checkboxes update a local count; they do not submit real approvals. Contact retains its existing copy-only behavior.

The sidebar becomes the existing accessible menu on smaller screens. Phone project rows keep the project and status together; the detail dialog retains the owner and next step. No continuous canvas or WebGL scene runs on this homepage.
